# Review affected contracts

Identify the intended behavior and changed contracts from the diff and consumer policy. Select
foundations, language, security, testing and application references only where their risks are affected.
Trace a suspected problem to reachable behavior and explain its consequence; distinguish a required
fix from a preference.

Check relevant verification evidence and its limits. Report actionable findings with locations,
triggering conditions and practical impact. Avoid claiming complete safety from an empty finding list
or inventing runtime evidence that was not observed.
